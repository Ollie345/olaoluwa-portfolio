IT audit and controls testing

Case studies built around fictional companies, so every name, person and figure in them is made up. Items marked Group 3 were produced with a team. Items marked Individual are my own.

IndividualRisk register

Risk register for an e-commerce business

Ten risks across the customer database, email, payment gateway, backups, laptops and cloud storage. Each risk has an asset, a vulnerability, a threat actor, an owner and the confidentiality, integrity or availability it affects. Likelihood and impact are scored 1 to 5, rated against a RAG matrix, then re-scored after the control is applied. Treatment is chosen as mitigate or transfer, with a plain-language action list for each.

The three critical risks were SQL injection against the customer database, a breach at the payment processor and failure of the backup environment. After controls they all land at medium. The two vendor risks are transferred through contract terms: yearly proof of PCI-DSS validation, a right-to-audit clause, breach notification deadlines and tested restores.

Group 3Control testing

Testing plan for new user access provisioning

A test plan for one control, new user access to Active Directory, written from the IT auditor's side. The plan covers the audit objective, the test of design and the test of operating effectiveness.

  • Inquiry with the control owner, inspection of the access policy and of sampled tickets, and re-performance of the approval.
  • The population is a full extract taken straight from the ticketing system, not a list the control owner has filtered.
  • Pass condition: every sampled request has a correct line manager request, an approval before provisioning, and permissions that match what was approved.
  • Failure condition: access granted with no request, approval from the wrong role, or permissions wider than approved. The recommendation is a second review step for high-privilege access and a look-back over access granted during the period.
IndividualWorkpaper

Entity level controls workpaper

A workpaper for a mid-sized company, testing two entity level controls: IT risk assessment, and monitoring of IT controls through internal audit. For each control it sets out the risk statement, the procedure, the approach and the expected evidence, then records findings, evidence and a conclusion with a rationale. Separate sheets hold the test of design and the test of operating effectiveness.

  • The risk assessment standard sets a minimum of two assessments a year, and the test checks that reports reached the risk committee.
  • For internal audit, the procedure checks the charter covers IT, the annual plan includes IT reviews, and one completed report shows exceptions raised and sent to stakeholders.

Odoo ERP modules

Custom modules I wrote or extended for Odoo 18 while working on ERP projects. The code belongs to the clients, so it is not published here. The screenshots come from my own local Odoo 18 install running these modules on demo data.

Telesales and routes

crm_telesales_custom

Adds routes to CRM for a field sales operation. Outlets are grouped into routes and zones, each route has a call schedule by day of the week with assigned agents, and every lead keeps its call history. Telesales agents see the outlets assigned to them. The lead form shows the route, the call count and the schedule.

Sales types and approval

ol_sales_custom

Extends Sales with local and export order types and an extra Review state. A local order goes from quotation to review, then a general manager approves or rejects it before it can move on. Export orders follow their own steps, built in the companion module export_nxp (export proceeds documents, allocations and shipment details).

Vendor cash plans

cash_plan

Splits one vendor bill into several scheduled payments. The plan holds the full amount and the bill reference. Each payment line is checked so the lines never add up to more than the bill.

Loan restructuring

account_loan_restructure

Adds restructure, pay-off and write-off requests to Odoo's loan app. A treasurer prepares the request, with a new amortisation schedule when the loan is restructured or a settlement account when it is paid off or written off. A CFO then approves or rejects it. Approval runs the accounting, and the chatter keeps the trail.

Manufacturing order approval

ol_mrp_custom

Adds Review, Approved and Rejected states to manufacturing orders. An order is sent for review, a manager approves or rejects it with a reason, and only approved orders can be confirmed.

REST API for CRM

custom_api

A JSON API for leads, opportunities, quotes and activities, so other systems can create and read CRM records. Requests carry a bearer token that is checked against Odoo API keys, and each call runs as the user who owns the key. The call below ran against my local install with a short-lived key.

Python and data analysis

Amazon product reviews

python/amazon-reviews-eda

Exploratory analysis of Amazon reviews from the Industrial and Scientific category. The script loads the review file, cleans missing and invalid rows, and picks three products with enough reviews. It then cleans the review text (stop words removed, words reduced to their roots) and reports the most common words, summary statistics, the rating distribution, reviews over time, a word cloud and a bubble chart for each product.

A second version does the same job as a set of small reusable functions with pandas doing the loading. It adds data quality checks, compares the words used in low and high ratings, and looks at which reviews get helpful votes.

amazon_reviews_eda.py and amazon_reviews_eda_functions.py

Shopping trends

python/shopping-trends

Two short scripts on a retail customer dataset of about 3,900 customers. One groups customers by number of previous purchases to show how many are repeat buyers. The other adds up purchase amounts by age band and labels each bar with the total. In that data the 46 to 55 band brings in the most revenue and the over 65 band the least.

purchase_frequency_histogram.py and revenue_by_age_histogram.py
